BUILD YOUR SENTENCES

Grammar Basics

我 + 是 + …
Sentence structure

Say who you are

Mandarin often uses a simple subject + verb + object pattern.

我是学生。 Wǒ shì xuésheng.
“I am a student.”

吗 ma
Questions

Turn a statement into a question

Add 吗 (ma) to the end of a statement to make a yes/no question.

你好吗？ Nǐ hǎo ma?
“How are you?”

不 bù
Negation

Say “not”

Put 不 (bù) before many verbs and adjectives to make them negative.

我不是老师。 Wǒ bú shì lǎoshī.
“I am not a teacher.” (不 changes tone before 是.)

的 de
Possession

Show who something belongs to

Use 的 (de) between a person and the thing they own or describe.

我的书 wǒ de shū
“My book.”
